# Changelog

All notable changes to the FloraDB dataset snapshots.

> Counts written with a `+` (e.g. `20,000+`) are **minimums**; the others are
> exact. A refresh can lower a figure as well as raise it: the 2026.11 edition
> has 683 enriched species (702 in 2026.10) and 124 plants with
> `toxicity_status = aspca_verified` (125 in 2026.10); see 2026-11-06 and
> 2026-11-07. Each entry's numbers describe the snapshot it was written for.

## Site update — 2026-11-08

- **Family pages' share summary** (`og:description`, the 28 family pages in all 5 languages): it said "N verified determinations with quantitative light/water parameters and ASPCA pet safety profiles", with N the family's plant count. The family pages are built from the free sample, and not every plant in it has an ASPCA determination: Acanthaceae has 6 plants in the sample, 3 of them without one, as the same page's description says. The summary now gives the family's plant count in the free sample and how many of those plants have an ASPCA pet-toxicity determination (species- or genus-level, as `/stats/` counts them), e.g. "6 houseplants in the free sample with quantitative light/water parameters; 3 of 6 with an ASPCA pet-toxicity determination." The one-plant families no longer read "1 verified determinations". `scripts/generate_seo_pages.py` writes it from the family's rows (`family_share_summary`) and names how many have light and watering values should a row ever lack them (all 100 have them today). Hand-translated for es/de/fr/pt-br (2 new segments per language); the retired segment is removed from `locales/*.json`. `sitemap.xml`: lastmod 2026-11-08 on the 140 changed pages. `i18n_common.py check`: 665 pages, 0 errors; every language publishes 133/133 pages.
- **Changelog header**: it said the live figures "only grow", so the numbers below "stay accurate between snapshots". A refresh can lower a figure too: the 2026.11 edition has 683 enriched species (702 in 2026.10) and 124 `aspca_verified` plants (125 in 2026.10). The header now says so, says that only counts written with a `+` are minimums, and says each entry's numbers describe the snapshot it was written for. No data or page change.
- **Pet-toxicity FAQ** (home page, page text and structured data, all 5 languages): it asked "Are pet toxicity statuses verified against veterinary clinical data?" and answered "Yes. Every plant entry is cross-referenced with the ASPCA…". In the 2026.11 care core, 101 of the 270 plants have no ASPCA determination and 45 are flagged toxic only because the ASPCA lists toxic members of their genus. The question is now "Where do the pet toxicity statuses come from?", and the answer says which records take the ASPCA entry for their species (`aspca_verified`), which are genus-level inferences (`aspca_genus_inferred`), that the rest are `unknown`, and that no veterinarian has reviewed FloraDB's matching or its genus-level inferences. The "What is FloraDB" answer no longer says "verified ASPCA dog and cat toxicity statuses", "field dataset" or "over 270" (the care core has 270 plants).
- **"GBIF-verified" is now "checked against GBIF"**, the wording adopted for the paid snapshot's own text on 2026-11-06: names are matched against the GBIF backbone, not verified by hand. Home page (description, share text, structured data, hero, method note, pricing list; the specimen card reads "GBIF-matched name"), `site.webmanifest`, `README.md`, `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`, the Kaggle description and column notes (`kaggle/dataset/dataset-metadata.json`), and `/stats/` ("re-checked against GBIF"). The README's "Verified tier" is now the "Species-level tier".
- **Family pages**: the description and profile said "20 of 22 verified toxic to pets" (Araceae) and "5 of 8" (Asparagaceae), counting plants that are toxic only by a genus-level inference as verified: 11 of Araceae's 20 and 1 of Asparagaceae's 5. They now say "toxic to pets", and the profile gives the genus-inferred count, e.g. "20 of 22 members toxic to pets (11 inferred from the ASPCA listing for the genus), 2 unverified." The description, profile and structured data said "in FloraDB" or "N determinations", but the counts are the free sample's (22 Araceae; the care core has 36): they now say "in the FloraDB free sample". The intro line no longer says "verified … specimens … clinical pet toxicity thresholds".
- **Plant and family directories** (`/plants/`, `/families/`): "Every houseplant profile in FloraDB … plus verified pet-toxicity status" and "Every botanical family in FloraDB" listed the 100 plants and 28 families of the free sample (the care core has 270 and 54). They now name the free sample and drop "verified". The portfolio's `scripts/generate_dir_hubs.py` writes them; its FloraDB text is updated to match.
- **Plant pages**: the structured data said "…and ASPCA dog/cat toxicity verification" on every plant, including the 15 with no determination. It now says, per plant, whether the toxicity comes from the ASPCA entry for the species, is inferred from the ASPCA listing for its genus, or has no ASPCA determination. The heading "Taxonomic & Field Verification" (no field work is done) is now "GBIF Taxonomy & Range".
- **`/stats/` labels**: "Toxic, ASPCA-verified" and "Non-toxic, ASPCA-verified" are now "… ASPCA species entry" (chart, table, definition, `data.json` and the chart SVG). The page was regenerated from the same 2026-11-06 snapshot: no figure changes.
- Translations: 36 new segments per language, hand-translated, then reviewed by a second translator whose fixes are applied (among them count phrases that broke when a count is 1, such as "1 non vérifiées"); the 33 catalog entries no page uses any more are removed (30 retired by these changes, 3 by earlier ones). `sitemap.xml`: lastmod 2026-11-08 on the 520 pages that changed (the family pages already carried it). `i18n_common.py check`: 665 pages, 0 errors; every language publishes 133/133 pages.

## Site update — 2026-11-07

- **Plant 50 (*Pilea involucrata*, Friendship Plant) no longer shows its genus's data**: GBIF matches the name only to the genus *Pilea*, so since the 2026.11 edition the full dataset gives this plant no GBIF key, vernacular name, range or photo. The free sample (`samples/floradb_sample.csv` and its Kaggle copy in `kaggle/dataset/`) still gave it the genus's key 2984417, the genus's English name "Clearweed", the genus-wide native range (first entry "Alabama") and the photo picked for the genus. Those seven fields are now blank on that row. No other row changes, and the sample stays edition 2026.07. Its care values and its ASPCA determination (non-toxic) are unchanged. The plant's page in all 5 languages no longer says "also known as Clearweed" or "native to Alabama", shows no photo and no GBIF button, and gives the GBIF usage key as "N/A".
- **Page generator** (`scripts/generate_seo_pages.py`): a row without an image or a GBIF URL now gets no photo box, no GBIF button and the site image as `og:image`. Before, it would have written an empty image `src`, an empty `og:image` and a GBIF button that linked back to the page itself. Every page that has a photo and a GBIF URL is generated byte for byte as before.
- **"A `gbif_source_url` on every row" reworded**: the data dictionary, the Kaggle description, `llms.txt` and the homepage method note (all 5 languages) now say the URL is there for each species-level match. The dictionary and the Kaggle column notes say when `gbif_usage_key` and `gbif_source_url` are blank.
- **Coverage figures re-measured on the 2026.11 care core** (README, data dictionary, Kaggle description): species-verified (ASPCA) 125 → 124 and `unknown` 100 → 101, since plant 219 lost the "non-toxic" it had inherited from its family (see 2026-11-06). English vernacular name 81% → 80% (216 of 270); the README labelled this figure "multilingual", but it counts English names. Two data-dictionary figures were already off before this edition: synonyms 22% → 11% (29 of 270 in the 2026.08, 2026.10 and 2026.11 cores) and image licence / commercial-use flag 99% → 98% (264 of 270). The README's Verified-tier line now says GBIF matches *Pilea involucrata* only to its genus.
- `sitemap.xml`: lastmod 2026-11-07 on the 10 changed pages (plant 50 and the home page, 5 languages each). `i18n_common.py check`: 665 pages, 0 errors; every language publishes 133/133 pages (2 new segments per language: the reworded method note and "N/A").
- **Kaggle update instructions** (`kaggle/README.md`, section 3): the `datasets version` command no longer ends in `-d`. In the Kaggle CLI that flag is `--delete-old-versions`: it deletes the dataset's older versions, not "remote files not present in the staging folder" as the README said. The command now runs from a one-word staging folder that holds the CSV and `dataset-metadata.json`, because `-p kaggle/dataset` stops with `Errno 2` on Windows (seen 2026-11-07): the CLI names its resume-cache file after the path of each uploaded file, and a `/` in it points into a cache folder that does not exist. The section also says what a version upload changes (the data file and the card's description, subtitle and keywords) and what it leaves alone (title, licence, column notes), and has the live card compared with `kaggle/dataset/dataset-metadata.json` before uploading, since the upload overwrites it. No data, page or site change.
- **More repository files off the website**: the site still served the Kaggle upload folder (`/kaggle/`), `examples/load_sample.py`, `assets/README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `SECURITY.md` and the i18n tool's marker file in each language folder (`/es/_i18n-generated.txt` and its `de`, `fr`, `pt-br` copies). They now answer the site's normal 404 page and stay available here on GitHub. The list also refuses `/notebooks/` and `/tests/` should this repository add them. Files the site links stay served: `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` (linked from `/stats/`), `LICENSE`, the free sample, `/stats/` and its `data.json`, `llms.txt` and the sitemap. No page or data changes (2026-11-07).

## Site update — 2026-11-06

- **Enriched species: 702 → 683** (home page in all 5 languages, `README.md`, `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`, `llms.txt`, Kaggle description). The ASPCA toxicity layer was re-linked to GBIF on 2026-11-03 under a stricter rule: a species record now links to GBIF only when GBIF matches it at species level, in the plant kingdom. 33 records lost their link: 32 that GBIF could place only in a genus, family, phylum or kingdom, and "Acantha", which it matched to a beetle genus. They keep that match as a label, with no GBIF key. The 20 enrichment rows that only those links reached were removed: they gave a plant the names, photo and range of its genus, family or kingdom. This count goes down, not up, because those rows never described the plant.
- **/stats/ regenerated from the 2026-11-06 snapshot** (all 5 languages; it showed the 2026-09-03 snapshot). Enriched species 702 → 683 (above). Care-core toxicity: plant 219 is now *Maranta cristata* with no determination, because its old "non-toxic" had been inferred from its family. So plants with an ASPCA determination 170 → 169, non-toxic 72 → 71, no determination 100 → 101, and the toxic share of determined plants 57.6% → 58.0% (98 plants, unchanged); Marantaceae has 3 determined plants instead of 4. ## 2026.07 — 2026-07-06

- Initial public snapshot.
- **270** curated houseplants with quantitative care metrics (Lux light thresholds, watering-day intervals, temperature and humidity ranges) across **54** botanical families.
- **891** ASPCA pet-toxicity records (dog / cat / horse) with clinical ingestion signs; **125** care plants species-verified and **45** conservatively genus-inferred.
- **20,000+** species GBIF taxonomic index; **702** species enriched with vernacular names, native range, and CC-licensed image references.
- Every record GBIF-verified with a `gbif_source_url`; per-record quality flags (`care_confidence`, `toxicity_status`, `image_commercial_safe`).
- Care metrics are category-normalized; toxicity is ASPCA-sourced or explicitly `unknown` — never guessed safe.

Full dataset & updates: [floradb](https://floradb.dataengineered.io)
