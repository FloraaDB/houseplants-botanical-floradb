How many common houseplants are toxic to cats or dogs

Of the 170 care-core houseplants with an ASPCA determination, 98 (57.6%) are toxic to cats or dogs: 53 verified against the ASPCA entry for the species and 45 inferred from an ASPCA entry for the genus. 72 are recorded as non-toxic. The other 100 of 270 plants have no determination and are excluded from the share, not assumed safe. In every determined record the cat and dog verdicts coincide.

What the ASPCA plant index says overall

Across the 859 ASPCA index entries with both a cat and a dog verdict, 373 (43.4%) are toxic to at least one of them: 364 to both, 8 to cats only and 1 to dogs only. 317 of the 694 entries with a horse verdict are toxic to horses. Among the largest families, every determined entry of Araceae is toxic; none of Arecaceae, Orchidaceae, Smilacaceae, Gesneriaceae, Piperaceae is.

Which plant families the houseplants come from

Araceae contributes the most plants to the care core: 36 of 270 (13.3%), and 31 of its 31 determined plants are toxic to cats or dogs. Asparagaceae follows with 17 and Crassulaceae with 16. The 15 largest families hold 67.8% of the plants; 54 families are represented in all.

How much light the houseplants need

Bright Indirect is the most common light requirement, 125 of 270 plants (46.3%), followed by Medium to Bright (86, 31.9%) and Direct Sun (37, 13.7%). The median lux band of a bright-indirect plant is 2,000 to 6,000 lux; of a direct-sun plant 5,000 to 15,000 lux, with a median watering interval of 18 days against 7 for bright-indirect plants.

How often the houseplants are watered

141 of 270 plants (52.2%) are on a 7-day watering interval; the median interval is 7 days and the range 2 to 21 days. 45 plants (16.7%) go 18 days or longer between waterings, 24 of them direct-sun plants.

Care requirements: medians and ranges

The median care-core houseplant wants 2,000 to 6,000 lux, water every 7 days, 16 to 30 °C and 55% humidity. 78 plants (28.9%) target 55% humidity; 55 (20.4%) target 30% or less.

Method, reuse and citation

  • Source. The full FloraDB snapshot of 2026-09-03: 270 care-core plants, 891 ASPCA index entries (crawled 2026-07-06), 20,000 GBIF-accepted species in the taxonomic index and 702 enriched species. Every row keeps its source URL and retrieval time; see the data dictionary.
  • Nothing is estimated. Toxicity is the ASPCA verdict for the species or, when every entry of the genus agrees, for the genus, and is otherwise NULL and excluded from the share. Care values are the curated guideline per plant. Where a denominator is smaller than 270, the section says so.
  • Not veterinary advice. Toxicity data is informational. If a pet ingests a plant, contact a veterinarian or ASPCA Animal Poison Control.
  • Refresh. The care core is re-verified against GBIF monthly; the ASPCA index is re-crawled on a manual, roughly quarterly cadence. This page and its charts are regenerated with each snapshot, so figures move. Cite the snapshot date.
  • Reuse. The figures and charts on this page are published under CC BY 4.0: use them in articles, slides and posts with a link to https://floradb.dataengineered.io/stats/. The machine-readable version is data.json. The underlying row-level dataset is a separate commercial product; a free 100-plant sample is in the public repository.
