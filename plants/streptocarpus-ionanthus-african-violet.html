SPECIMEN NO. #35 🟢 PET SAFE Family: Gesneriaceae

Streptocarpus ionanthus

Common Name: African Violet

African Violet (Streptocarpus ionanthus) is a houseplant in the Gesneriaceae family, native to Chagos Archipelago. It is a medium to bright-light species that thrives between 1000 and 3500 lux. Optimal indoor care calls for watering roughly every 7 days, temperatures of 18–26°C, and about 60% humidity. According to ASPCA data, it is non-toxic and considered pet-safe for cats and dogs. It is also known as African Violet.
